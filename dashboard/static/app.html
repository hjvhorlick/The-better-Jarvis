Only needed for online text answers. Local commands and saved knowledge continue to work without a key.
Suppliers — what JARVIS can go and fetch (optional keys, nothing to do with Gemini). Each supplier hands over a different kind of file, and together they cover pictures, video, sound and books. Paste a key and that supplier moves to the front of the queue for the kinds it covers. Leave them all empty and JARVIS still fetches every kind from the suppliers that need nothing at all.
Reading the supplier list…
Why the keys are optional: Pexels and Pixabay have no audio endpoint in their public APIs — that is in both sets of documentation — so music comes from Jamendo, or from Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and the Internet Archive when there is no client ID. Books come from Project Gutenberg, which needs no key because the whole catalogue is public domain. A missing key narrows the choice of supplier; it never stops a search. Register your own supplier — any website with a JSON API, or one of your own applications. Nothing here needs a restart, and removing one is one click.

Clear Glass & Yellow Lines — every surface
The glass is clear: you look through it at the room, or at your own camera when it is on, and the thin yellow line is what tells you where a pane ends. Three kinds of surface, two sliders each. Transparency: 0% is invisible glass, 90% is a smoked pane. Sand-blast: a little frost cleans the picture up; a lot of sand makes the pane milky, so nothing behind it can be read — private, not transparent. The yellow line and the bloom (the glow that lifts a pane off the dark) are set once, for all of them, and your numbers are remembered on this computer.
