Clear Glass & Yellow Lines — every surface
The glass is clear: you look through it at the room, or at your own camera when it is on, and the thin yellow line is what tells you where a pane ends. Three kinds of surface, two sliders each. Transparency: 0% is invisible glass, 90% is a smoked pane. Sand-blast: a little frost cleans the picture up; a lot of sand makes the pane milky, so nothing behind it can be read — private, not transparent. The yellow line and the bloom (the glow that lifts a pane off the dark) are set once, for all of them, and your numbers are remembered on this computer.
